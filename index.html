About

Where the strings lead, the mist follows

From Berlin’s Museum Island to the Nevada desert, Chengyu Wang carries an old Chinese landscape wherever she sets down her guzheng.

She first sat down at the guzheng as a small child. Today, based in Berlin, she plays traditional pieces such as Xiangshan She Gu at the Brandenburg Gate, on international trade-fair stages, in company offices and at private gatherings. Every performance is an invitation to slow down and travel, for a few minutes, into that landscape.
